DESCRIPTION
It's Saturday at the library, and for these kids the real challenge is just getting started. ♟️📚

At chess club, the pieces start out as a mystery. Which way does the knight move? Why can't the bishop go straight? So they learn: one piece, one rule, one move at a time. And slowly, something changes. They slow down, think three moves ahead, and sit with a tough position instead of giving up.

Every game is a lesson in patience, focus and sportsmanship. Win, and they shake hands. Lose, and they shake hands. Then they set up the board and play again. These are skills that follow them back to the classroom, and into life.
