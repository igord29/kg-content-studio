**TITLE**
Saturday Chess at the Library | Community Literacy Club

**DESCRIPTION**
Saturday mornings look a little different at the library. Most of these kids had never touched a chess piece before joining Community Literacy Club — and now they're learning patience, focus, and sportsmanship one piece and one rule at a time. Win or lose, they shake hands, set the board up, and go again. Skills like that don't stay at the chess table; they carry straight into the classroom.

Community Literacy Club is a New York youth nonprofit (est. 2020) using tennis, chess, and reading to build the next generation of confident, curious learners.

🌐 Learn more and support our programs: https://CommunityLiteracyClub.org
