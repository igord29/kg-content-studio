# UnitedSets Thanksgiving Day: 10 viral highlight Shorts

These are 10 vertical (9:16) MP4s, about 20–30 seconds each, for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels. They are built for engagement and replays, and they don't promote any tournament.

## How each Short is edited

The source is one camera, the full match recording of a June UnitedSets point. Each Short runs in this order:

1. **Hook (0–2 s).** The best moment of the point in slow motion, a glitch-in, big hook text and an emoji sticker.
2. **The point.** It plays from serve to finish and cuts between a wide court view and tight punch-ins on the near player.
   - On every big swing: a whip transition into a slow-motion punch-in with a small camera shake and a flash, then a cut back to wide.
   - A live **RALLY 0:xx** timer runs in the corner, and a **SLOW-MO** tag shows on the slowed shots.
3. **The finish.** The last big hit of the rally in super slow motion, then a freeze frame with 🔥.
4. **Rewind ⏪.** The point scrubs backwards with scanlines and a colour split.
5. **Replay.** The hook moment again, tighter and slower, with the analyst's line.
6. **Sting (1.5 s).** UnitedSets logo plus "FOLLOW FOR MORE". It's kept short so the video loops back into the hook.

Commentary appears as captions: the play-by-play call and Jessica, the analyst. Each video also keeps the real court sound. There's no music, so add a trending sound in the app if you like, kept low under the voices.

**Rules followed:** no player names (players are described by shirt colour), no price, and no tournament promo.
